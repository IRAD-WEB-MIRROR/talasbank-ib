Rules of remote banking service of Talasbank (Bank of Talas) OJSC for private clients. Approved by the Management Board, minutes No. 41 of 25.08.2014, as amended by minutes No. 17 of 22.03.2021 (SMS codes in place of paper code tables). In force from 01.04.2021.

1. General provisions

1.1. These Rules are a public offer of Talasbank (Bank of Talas) Open Joint-Stock Company, licence of the Bank of Irad No. B-001 (the Bank), to private clients holding a current or card account with the Bank (the Client), to use the remote banking system Talasbank Online at ib.talasbank.net and the application Talasbank Mobile (the System).

1.2. The Client accepts the offer by signing the application on form IB-1 at a branch of the Bank. From that day the Rules, the Tariffs of Talasbank for private clients (section 4) and the general conditions of the Client's account agreement form one agreement between the Client and the Bank.

1.3. The Rules are published on this page and kept on the information board of every branch. The Bank may change the Rules by publishing the new text on this page and in Notices not less than 10 calendar days before the change. If the Client continues to use the System after that day, the Client is deemed to have accepted the change.

2. Access to the System

2.1. The Bank gives the Client a login in a sealed envelope at the branch. The first password is sent by SMS to the telephone number registered at the branch within 3 working days. Each entry is confirmed by a one-time code sent by SMS.

2.2. An order confirmed by the Client's login, password and SMS code has the same force as an order on paper signed by the Client.

2.3. After two wrong SMS codes in a row the System blocks access for 24 hours. Access is restored at the branch on form IB-3.

2.4. The Client changes the telephone number for SMS codes only in person at the branch, with the identity card or passport.

3. Operations

3.1. Through the System the Client may view balances and statements; transfer money between own accounts; transfer to other clients of the Bank and to other banks of Irad through the Clearing House of the Bank of Irad; pay for utilities and the services listed on the payments page.

3.2. Orders to other banks received before 15:00 on a working day are executed the same day; later orders, on the next working day. Transfers between accounts in som and US dollars are made at the official rate of the Bank of Irad on the day of the transfer.

3.3. Limits on operations are published under Tariffs and limits. Limits set by notices of the Bank of Irad apply whatever the channel.

3.4. The Bank refuses an order if the balance is not enough to pay it with the commission, if the details are incomplete, or if the order breaks the law or the instructions of the Bank of Irad.

4. Commissions

4.1. The Client pays commissions under section 4 of the Tariffs of Talasbank for private clients in force on the day of the operation. The commission is debited from the account from which the payment is made.

4.2. Operations listed as without commission on the page Tariffs and limits are free.

5. Security and responsibility

5.1. The Client keeps the login, password and SMS codes secret and does not give them to anyone, including employees of the Bank. The Bank never asks for them.

5.2. The Client informs the Bank at once by calling 1993 if the telephone, the SIM card or the password is lost or may be known to someone else. The Bank blocks access within one hour of the call.

5.3. The Bank is not responsible for losses from orders confirmed with the Client's password and SMS code before the Client's call, nor for delays caused by failures of communication lines and electricity supply outside its control.

5.4. The Bank keeps a record of entries to the System and of every order. The record is evidence in disputes.

6. Disputes and termination

6.1. The Client may dispute an operation in writing at the branch within 30 days of the operation. The Bank answers within 15 working days.

6.2. Disputes not settled by negotiation are heard by the courts of the ISEZ at the place of the Bank's head office.

6.3. The Client may stop using the System at any time by an application at the branch. The agreement ends when the Client's last account is closed.

7. Deposits

7.1. Money on the Client's accounts is not insured by a deposit guarantee fund. The Bank keeps the reserves and capital ratios required by the Bank of Irad.
