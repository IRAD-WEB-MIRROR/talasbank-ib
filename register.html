How to register

Talasbank Online is available to private clients who have a current account in som or US dollars at Talasbank and a mobile phone number of Attelkom or Torgoy. Companies and private entrepreneurs connect with form IB-2.

Step 1. Come to the branch

Come to the branch where your account is kept, with your passport (for foreign citizens: passport and residence permit). Branches in Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl connect clients on working days until 16:00. If you have no account yet, it is opened at the same visit.

Step 2. Fill in form IB-1

The operator fills in the application form IB-1 with you: your accounts, the mobile number for SMS codes, the code word for the information service. Check the mobile number carefully. You sign two copies; one stays with you.

Step 3. The envelope

You receive your login in a sealed envelope. Do not accept an envelope that has been opened. The first password is sent by SMS within 3 working days (usually the next day after 10:00).

Step 4. First login

  1. Open https://ib.talasbank.net/ (type the address yourself).
  2. Enter the login from the envelope, the password from the SMS and the code from the picture.
  3. Enter the one-time code that comes by SMS (6 digits, valid for 3 minutes).
  4. Change the password: 8 to 12 characters, letters and digits; it must not repeat the last five passwords.
  5. Check that you see your accounts. If an account is missing, tell your branch.

Companies (form IB-2)

The director or a person with a power of attorney brings form IB-2 with the seal of the company, the charter, the certificate of registration and the card with sample signatures. Payment orders in Talasbank Online are signed with the SMS code of each person named in the form IB-2. Connection takes up to 5 working days.

What you can do in Talasbank Online

  • see balances and statements (also in Excel);
  • transfer between your own accounts in som and US dollars;
  • transfer to other clients of Talasbank and, through the Clearing House, to other banks of Irad (orders received after 15:00 are executed the next working day);
  • pay NurGaz, Talas Electric Networks and the city water service;
  • block your card.

Not available: cash, transfers abroad, Tulpar Pay, deposits (open them at the branch).
