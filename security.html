Talasbank never asks for your password, the code from the picture or the SMS code. Not by telephone, not by SMS, not by e-mail, not in Tulpar, not in person. Anyone who asks for them is a fraudster, even if he knows your name, your card number and your balance.

How fraudsters work (cases of 2025–2026)

  1. «Security service». A call or a Tulpar message: «A suspicious payment from your account. Tell us the code to cancel it.» The code they ask for confirms their payment.
  2. False site. A link to a page that looks like this one, at an address such as ib-talasbank.net, talasbank-online.com or ib.talasbank.net.kz. The only address of Talasbank Online is https://ib.talasbank.net/.
  3. Dollars without the limit. «We will change your som into cash dollars above the US$300 limit» or «into Tulparcoin», for a transfer to a «bank employee». Talasbank does not sell dollars outside its cash desks and does not work with Tulpar Pay (notice of 09.10.2026).
  4. New SIM card. Someone asks for a duplicate of your SIM card at a phone shop and receives your SMS codes. If your phone suddenly loses the network, call your operator and the information service 1993 at once.
  5. «Your relative is in trouble». A call asking for money for a relative at the police or in hospital, to be sent at once. Call the relative yourself first.

Rules

  • Type the address ib.talasbank.net yourself. Do not open the system from links in messages.
  • Use the virtual keyboard on computers that are not yours. Do not enter the system in internet cafés.
  • Do not keep the password in the phone or on paper next to the computer.
  • Read the SMS before you type the code: it names the sum and the recipient. If they are not yours, do not type the code.
  • Tell your branch at once when you change your mobile number.
  • After work press «Exit» and close the browser.

If you have given your code to someone

Call the information service 1993 at any time (24 hours): the operator will block access to Talasbank Online and your cards. Then come to your branch with your passport and write an application (form IB-3 to unblock; a complaint about an unauthorised payment within 15 days). Complaints are answered within 15 working days.

You can also report fraud to the police at your district police station.
