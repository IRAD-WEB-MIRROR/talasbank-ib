Tariffs and limits

Commissions for operations in Talasbank Online are set by section 4 of the Tariffs of Talasbank for private clients, approved by the Board. The tariffs are on the information board of every branch and are given by the information service 1993. For companies: the Tariffs for legal entities, section 6, at your branch.

Without commission

  • viewing balances and statements;
  • transfers between your own accounts in som and US dollars (at the official rate of the Bank of Irad on the day of the transfer);
  • payments to NurGaz, Talas Electric Networks and the city water service (notice of 15.06.2022);
  • blocking a card.

By the Tariffs of the bank

  • transfers to other clients of Talasbank;
  • transfers to other banks of Irad through the Clearing House;
  • connection of a company (form IB-2) and the monthly service of companies;
  • a new envelope with login (if lost).

Limits for private clients

  • transfers to other clients and other banks: up to 50,000 som a day and 300,000 som a month;
  • transfers in US dollars to other clients: up to US$1,000 a day;
  • transfers between your own accounts: not limited;
  • cash in US dollars at cash desks and cash machines: US$300 a month per client (notice of 05.05.2026), whatever the channel.

To raise a limit, write an application at your branch. Limits for companies are set in the agreement.

Hours

Talasbank Online works 24 hours, except the weekly maintenance on Sunday 01:00–05:00 Irad Time (see Maintenance). Orders to other banks received before 15:00 on a working day are executed the same day; later orders, the next working day.
