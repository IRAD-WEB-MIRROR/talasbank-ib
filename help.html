How to connect

Talasbank Online is available to private clients with a current account in som or US dollars and a mobile phone number of Attelkom or Torgoy.

  1. Come to the branch where your account is kept, with your passport.
  2. Fill in the application form IB-1 (connection) and, for companies, form IB-2 with the seal of the company.
  3. You will receive your login in a sealed envelope. The first password is sent by SMS within 3 working days.
  4. At the first login change the password. The password must have 8 to 12 characters, letters and digits, and must not repeat the last five passwords.

Forgot your password?

For security reasons the password cannot be restored by telephone or e-mail. Visit your branch with your passport and fill in form IB-3. A new password is sent by SMS within 3 working days.

Demo version

The demo version is temporarily unavailable during the modernisation of card processing.

System requirements

Internet Explorer 8.0 or higher (recommended), Mozilla Firefox 3.6 or higher. Screen resolution 1024×768. JavaScript and cookies must be enabled. Other browsers are not supported and the system may work incorrectly.

Errors

1046 Session expired. Log in again.
2203 The authorisation server did not respond in time. Try again in a few minutes.
0x0417 Access blocked after three unsuccessful attempts. Visit your branch with form IB-3.
